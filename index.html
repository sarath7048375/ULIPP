Product-credit rules captured

  • Single Premium & Top-up count as 10%.
  • Riders are treated as their base plan.
  • Cancer Protection / Term policies sold to the same customer in the same month are treated as Rider.
  • If L1 does minimum 2 Cancer NOPs in a month, full Rider credit applies even if minimum rider-premium condition is not met.
  • L2s, BMs and RMs receive the same product credit as the L1 for that policy.

Source: PDF page 21. The company reserves the right to change the grid.
